## 1. Introduction

NxtStepGo is an educational guidance portal designed to help undergraduate and postgraduate science and engineering students choose the right career path after college. The portal provides comprehensive, verified information across five specialized engineering and scientific disciplines:

* Chemical Engineering
* Biotechnology
* Bioinformatics
* Bioengineering and Nanotechnology
* Chemistry

Whether a student intends to pursue higher academic degrees (M.Tech or Ph.D.), prepare for national competitive exams, enter the private industrial workforce, or launch a technology startup, NxtStepGo organizes all resources into a single, intuitive interface.

---

## 2. Navigating the Platform

### 2.1 Guest Access vs. Registered Student Accounts

NxtStepGo offers two tiers of access:

1. **Open Guest Browsing (No Account Required):**
   * Any student, parent, or counselor can immediately browse all departmental data, university programs, competitive exams, job roles, and startup directories.
   * Guest users can also test their qualifications using the Skill Matcher diagnostic tool.
   * No registration or sign-in is required for research.

2. **Registered Student Account (Free):**
   * Students who create an account unlock personal saving features.
   * Save individual university programs, jobs, and exams to a personal Bookmarks dashboard.
   * Organize target opportunities into custom collections (e.g., "Top GATE College Choices" or "Fall 2027 Placement Applications").

## 3. Step-by-Step User Workflows

### 3.1 Step 1: Selecting an Academic Department

1. Navigate to the homepage at `http://localhost:5000/`.
2. Click the blue **"Explore Branches"** button.
3. Choose your field of study from the five available department cards:
   * Chemical Engineering
   * Biotechnology
   * Bioinformatics
   * Bioengineering
   * Chemistry
4. Clicking a department opens that branch's central **Career Hub**.

### 3.2 Step 2: Exploring Higher Education Programs

1. From the Department Hub, select **"PG Programs"** or **"Ph.D Programs"**.
2. Browse curated lists of Indian institutions (including IITs, NITs, Central Universities, and premier research centers).
3. Review key program details:
   * Degree title (M.Tech, M.S., or Ph.D.)
   * Program specialization
   * Location (City and State)
   * Entrance examination requirements (e.g., GATE, CSIR-NET, BITS HD)
   * Official university website link

### 3.3 Step 3: Preparing for Competitive Examinations

1. From the Department Hub, select **"Exams"**.
2. View detailed profiles of relevant national and institutional examinations (such as GATE, CSIR-UGC NET, ICMR JRF, DBT-BET, and UPSC Engineering Services).
3. Review critical dates, eligibility criteria, and direct links to official syllabus documents.

### 3.4 Step 4: Reviewing Industry Placements and Job Descriptions

1. From the Department Hub, select **"Jobs"**.
2. Browse active industry roles matching your academic background.
3. Review:
   * Core responsibilities
   * Required technical skills (such as ASPEN Plus, Python, HPLC, or Bioreactor Design)
   * Typical industry sectors hiring for the role

### 3.5 Step 5: Running the AI Skill Matcher

The Skill Matcher helps students understand which industry careers match their current skills and highlights the exact competencies needed to become competitive.

1. Open the **Skill Matcher** from the top navigation bar or Department Hub.
2. Enter your current technical capabilities in one of two ways:
   * **Free-Text Diagnostic:** Type a natural-language description (e.g., *"I have experience in Python, data analysis, and basic machine learning"*). The system will automatically identify your skills.
   * **Skill Selection:** Choose your skills from the searchable skills catalog.
3. Click **"Find Matching Jobs"**.
4. The diagnostic report displays:
   * **Match Confidence Percentage:** Calculated compatibility for each industrial role.
   * **Your Matching Skills:** Highlighted competencies that you already possess.
   * **Skills to Develop:** Missing skills needed for the role, paired with curated learning resource links.

## 4. Managing Bookmarks and Collections

### 4.1 Creating an Account and Signing In

1. Click **"Sign Up"** in the top navigation bar.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and a password of at least 6 characters.
3. Click **"Create Account"**. You will be logged in immediately.
4. If you already have an account, click **"Login"** and enter your registered credentials.

### 4.2 Bookmarking Opportunities

1. When viewing any Program, Job, Exam, or Startup card, click the **"Bookmark"** button.
2. A confirmation notification indicates the item has been added to your profile.
3. To view all saved resources, click your name in the top navigation bar and select **"Bookmarks"**.
4. Filter saved bookmarks using the category tabs (All, Programs, Jobs, Exams, Startups).
5. Click **"Remove"** on any card to delete it from your bookmarks.

### 4.3 Building Personal Collections

1. Click your name in the top navigation bar and select **"My Collections"**.
2. Under **"Create New Collection"**, enter a name (e.g., *"IIT Madras Application Shortlist"*) and an optional description.
3. Click **"Create Collection"**.
4. Group related items into your collection to track application deadlines and preparation roadmaps.

---

## 5. Security and Account Tips

* **Password Protection:** Never share your account password. NxtStepGo encrypts all passwords using enterprise standards before storage.
* **Signing Out:** If using a shared university terminal or library workstation, click your name in the top right corner and click **"Logout"** to clear your local session securely.
